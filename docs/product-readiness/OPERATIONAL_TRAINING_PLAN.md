# OPERATIONAL TRAINING PLAN

## Training Objective

Enable each operational role to execute daily and weekly responsibilities consistently before controlled launch.

## Target Trainees

- Owner
- Platform manager/admin
- Sector manager
- Tiger Care agents
- Moderators
- Jordan sector teams

## Role-Based Training Tracks

### Owner Track

- Governance checkpoints
- Go/No-Go decision discipline
- Risk and escalation interpretation

### Platform Manager Track

- Cross-sector coordination
- Access control governance
- Incident and SLA oversight

### Sector Manager Track

- Sector queue triage
- Listing quality and policy alignment
- Escalation and staffing handoff

### Tiger Care Track

- Intake/classification/priority workflow
- Response templates
- 24h response SLA
- Privacy-safe communication

### Moderator Track

- Report reasons and evidence handling
- Action ladder (warning to ban)
- Appeal handling and decision logging

### Jordan Teams Track

- Launch-hour operations
- Shift handover protocol
- Incident channel practice

## Daily Procedures Training

- Queue opening routine
- SLA check routine
- Escalation triggers
- End-of-shift handoff notes

## Weekly Procedures Training

- KPI and incident review
- Policy drift check
- Role coverage and staffing check
- Improvement actions with owner visibility

## Practical Exercises

- Simulated listing rejection with reason quality check
- Simulated Tiger Care urgent ticket escalation
- Simulated access-denied role boundary scenario
- Simulated moderation appeal flow

## Competency Test Per Role

For each role, evaluate:

- Policy knowledge accuracy
- Task execution correctness
- Communication quality
- Escalation judgment
- Evidence and logging discipline

Result options:

- `Pass`
- `Conditional Pass`
- `Re-Training Required`
