# PRODUCT SCOPE FREEZE

## Platform Direction

- VVIP TIGER is one unified platform identity.
- Sector choice is a classification filter, not a separate app.
- Jordan-first controlled launch direction stays active.

## Three Sectors As Filters

- Auto parts and services.
- Materials and supplies.
- Real estate.

## Unified Base Account Rule

- One base account per user.
- Sectors and operational permissions do not create separate accounts.
- The same account is used for Home, Marketplace, Search, and profile access.
- Sector filters shape browsing and listings, not account creation.

## Owner-Approved Launch And Monetization Rules

- Four months are free from registration.
- Publisher accounts move to a future monthly subscription after the free period.
- Buyer and browsing accounts remain free.
- Annual billing may be considered later.
- Subscription amount is not authorized in this mission.
- Commission is 0%.
- No in-platform payments are included in the 60-day launch.
- No paid subscriptions are included in the 60-day launch.
- No commissions, settlement, payment gateway, or escrow are included in the 60-day launch.
- Monetization decisions remain post-launch owner decisions.
- VVIP TIGER remains a discovery and private-contact platform only.

## Business, Retention, And Deletion Rules

- Business verification is required according to the approved business registration policy.
- Conversation retention is 90 days after last activity.
- Account deletion includes immediate disablement, a 30-day recovery period, then permanent deletion or anonymization.
- The difference between disablement and final deletion must remain explicit.

## Deferred And Rejected Items

- Video in listings is rejected.
- Group chat is rejected.
- Public broadcast channels are rejected.
- Platform participation in contract, payment, delivery, or guarantee is rejected.
- Any claim that frontend-only controls are backend authorization is rejected.
- Exact framework choice remains deferred.
- Country expansion beyond Jordan remains deferred.

## Phase Integrity Lock

- `P08` remains `INCOMPLETE`.
- `P09` remains `NOT STARTED`.
- This freeze is requirement alignment only, not backend delivery authorization.
