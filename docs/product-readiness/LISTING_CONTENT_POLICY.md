# LISTING CONTENT POLICY

## Publishing Limits

- Maximum `4` listings per account per week.
- Maximum `7` images per listing.
- Images only. Video is not allowed.
- Administrative exception may be granted only by owner decision.

## Image Standards

- Target ratio: `4:3`.
- Allowed editing actions: crop, zoom, reposition, rotate, reorder, set cover.
- Process requirement: discard original capture and store processed image only.

## Price Rule

- Price is required.
- Price must be greater than `0`.
- Decimal prices are allowed.

## Retention Rule

- Listing and listing images are deleted after `120` days according to platform policy.

## Data Structure Guidance

- Structured listing data should be about 80%.
- Short free text should be about 20%.
- The balance is intended to keep search quality high without removing human context.

## Listing Lifecycle States

- `draft`
- `pending`
- `approved`
- `rejected`
- `active`
- `closed`
- `expired`
- `deleted`

## Review And Rejection Reasons

### Typical Rejection Reasons

- Missing mandatory fields.
- Price invalid (`0`, negative, or non-numeric).
- Media violates image-only rule.
- Misleading or prohibited content.
- Wrong category/sector selection.
- Duplicate/spam behavior.

### Review Handling

- `pending`: waiting for moderation review.
- `approved`: eligible for active exposure.
- `rejected`: returned with reason and allowed correction path.

## Boundary Statement

This policy is product and operations guidance.
It does not claim current backend enforcement by itself.
