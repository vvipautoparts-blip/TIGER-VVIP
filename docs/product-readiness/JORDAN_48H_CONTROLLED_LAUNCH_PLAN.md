# JORDAN 48H CONTROLLED LAUNCH PLAN

## Purpose

Controlled, limited launch rehearsal in Jordan with strict participant and incident controls.

This plan does not claim current production readiness.

## Pre-Launch Preparation

- Confirm approved participant list.
- Confirm test-only dataset for early window.
- Confirm Tiger Care staffing window.
- Confirm incident channel and escalation tree.
- Confirm rollback and stop criteria communication.

## Authorized Participants

- Owner representatives
- Platform operations lead
- Sector leads (auto parts, materials, real estate)
- Tiger Care operations team
- Moderation duty owner

## Operating Window

- Total window: 48 hours.
- Define active monitoring shifts by hour.
- Assign clear on-call owners for each shift.

## First Stage Data Rule

- Start with test data only.
- Expand to limited real usage only after explicit Go checkpoint.

## Incident And Fault Channel

- Single incident channel with timestamped log.
- Severity levels: Sev1, Sev2, Sev3.
- Explicit handoff rules between shifts.

## Tiger Care Readiness

- 24-hour response commitment in effect.
- Priority routing for urgent requests.
- Escalation path active for severe cases.

## Performance Monitoring

- Track critical page availability and responsiveness.
- Track listing flow completion health.
- Track Tiger Care request handling latency.

## Go / No-Go Checkpoints

- Checkpoint 1: after first 6 hours.
- Checkpoint 2: at 24 hours.
- Final checkpoint: at 48 hours.

Any checkpoint may trigger `No-Go` if severe unresolved incidents remain.

## Stop And Rollback Mechanism

- Immediate stop for critical safety, data, or abuse incidents.
- Revert to safe controlled mode per incident runbook.
- Preserve evidence and incident timeline before changes.

## End Of 48h Report

- Participant summary
- Incident summary by severity
- SLA and support response summary
- Product behavior gaps
- Owner decision proposals for next step

## Mandatory Statement

No statement in this plan should be interpreted as production-ready approval at this time.
