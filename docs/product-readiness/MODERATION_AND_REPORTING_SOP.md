# MODERATION AND REPORTING SOP

## 1. Prohibited Content

- Fraud and scam attempts.
- Identity impersonation.
- Abuse, harassment, or hate language.
- Fake products or deceptive pricing.
- Illegal or policy-prohibited goods/services.

## 2. Reporting Intake

- Report enters `pending review` queue.
- Required fields: listing/report reference, reason, reporter context, timestamp.

## 3. Report Reasons (Standard)

- Fraud / scam
- Impersonation
- Abuse / harassment
- Fake product
- Misleading price
- Wrong category
- Spam / duplicate
- Other (with explanation)

## 4. Decision Ladder

- `warning`
- `restriction`
- `suspension`
- `ban`

Action must match severity, recurrence, and evidence quality.

## 5. Evidence Protection

- Preserve submitted evidence and moderation notes before final action.
- Keep immutable references where possible.
- Do not delete evidence without an approved retention policy.

## 6. Appeal Flow

- User may submit appeal with supporting evidence.
- Appeal reviewed by a different moderator or higher authority.
- Final decision logged with rationale.

## 7. Moderator Authority Boundaries

- Moderator can review and action within policy matrix.
- Moderator cannot change owner-level governance or legal policy.
- Moderator cannot permanently remove evidence outside approved process.

## 8. Operating Notes

- Keep user-facing communication concise and respectful.
- Avoid disclosing private internal reasoning that risks security abuse.
- Escalate legal/safety-sensitive incidents immediately.
