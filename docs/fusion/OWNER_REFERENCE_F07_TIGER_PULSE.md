# OWNER REFERENCE — F07 TIGER Pulse

**Current parent authority:** `VVIP TIGER FUSION 2026 — FINAL Owner Constitution`

**Subsystem:** F07 — TIGER Pulse / Hero Dynamic Ad Ribbon Engine

**Owner status:** APPROVED FOR FUSION ROADMAP — implementation follows F00–F06 dependency closure.

## Owner intent

TIGER Pulse is a premium, lightweight, in-platform campaign network embedded in the same VVIP TIGER Single Surface. It must deliver sponsored campaigns without clutter, expand into an interactive brochure experience on demand, support global/country/sector/cross-sector targeting, and remain governed by privacy, SCG permissions, V13/DIDE delivery economics, Global Money Fabric, accessibility, weak-network adaptation, and Launch Passport evidence.

## Non-negotiable owner rules

- no noisy permanent ticker that harms reading;
- no separate advertising platform/skin for owner or partners;
- same product surface for users, partners, employees, and owner;
- campaign management appears only through server-confirmed SCG capabilities;
- sponsor campaigns may come from inside or outside listing sectors;
- micro creative must be lightweight and adapt to weak/data-saver networks;
- HD brochure loads on demand;
- uploaded PDFs are quarantined/scanned and rendered/sanitized before interactive display;
- campaign content is visibly labeled sponsored/promotional;
- no sensitive-personal-attribute targeting without explicit future legal/privacy approval;
- no browser role/country claim controls campaign authority or billing;
- no platform checkout/shipping/escrow is introduced by a campaign CTA without a separately approved commerce constitution;
- V13 qualified-impression truth remains authoritative for billable delivery;
- no campaign/package may be sold when pricing/cost/FX/country evidence implies an unverified or known negative margin;
- all campaign mutations are audited;
- campaign capabilities obey Sovereign Capability Graph delegation limits;
- F07 must pass security, performance, finance, Arabic/English, accessibility, mobile, Red-Team, Digital Twin, and Launch Passport gates before global launch eligibility.

## Owner experience

From the same `⋮` capability gateway, an authorized owner/partner can access scoped campaign functions such as:

- campaigns;
- creatives/brochures;
- targeting;
- schedule;
- budget/delivery state;
- countries/regions;
- sectors/cross-sector/global reach;
- analytics;
- evidence/audit;
- pause/suspend actions within granted authority.

No partner can grant or operate campaign authority outside the exact capabilities/countries/sectors/resources delegated by the owner.

## User experience

`Micro Ribbon -> Open Brochure -> Interact/Contact -> Close -> Return to same context`

Primary micro-ribbon content:

- sponsor mark;
- short headline;
- optional country/sector badge;
- sponsored label;
- one concise CTA.

Expanded brochure can support:

- single or multiple sanitized pages;
- swipe/page navigation;
- zoom;
- on-demand HD;
- Contact/Call/WhatsApp where policy permits;
- validated external website;
- Save/Share;
- sanitized brochure download where authorized.

## Performance truth

Do not promise microsecond delivery or universal fixed byte sizes. Separate and measure:

- perceived UI latency;
- campaign metadata latency;
- micro-creative delivery latency;
- brochure HD delivery latency;
- interaction latency.

Weak-network/data-saver mode may force static creative, lower-weight derivative, no auto-rotation, and no HD prefetch.

## Financial truth

TIGER Pulse is a revenue surface, but financial behavior remains fail-closed:

- qualified impression rules from V13;
- frequency caps and fraud filtering;
- weighted fair delivery within purchased entitlement and capacity;
- double-entry ledger where money is recognized;
- idempotent campaign/financial events;
- no AI autonomous money movement;
- `PACKAGE_NOT_SELLABLE` / equivalent block when costs, FX, taxes/fees, capacity, or margin evidence are incomplete.
