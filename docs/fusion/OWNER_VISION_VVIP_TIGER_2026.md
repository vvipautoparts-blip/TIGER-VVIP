## 3. AI-assisted attack resistance and reverse-engineering posture

The platform does **not** claim to be impossible to hack or impossible for AI to analyze.

The engineering target is **AI-assisted attack resistance through minimum exposed authority**.

### 3.1 Private Core

The client must never be authoritative for:

- money calculations/ledger mutation;
- OWNER authority;
- SCG delegation validation;
- campaign billing truth;
- country activation;
- security kill-switch state;
- privileged policy transitions.

The browser/app receives only the minimum data and decisions needed for the current user action.

### 3.2 Minimum Truth

Sensitive internal decision logic is kept server-side. Client responses expose bounded result data and stable error/status codes, not unnecessary internal rules, keys, private scoring details, secrets, or raw privileged records.

### 3.3 Obfuscation/Wasm rule

Rust/Wasm/native compilation may be used when it provides measurable performance, portability, memory-safety, or IP-friction benefits. It is **not** treated as an authorization/security boundary by itself.

Security remains based on server authorization, cryptographic binding, RLS, least privilege, short-lived capability, audit, rate limits, recovery, and release integrity.

### 3.4 Audit evidence

Sensitive owner/financial/campaign/delegation actions generate append-only evidence appropriate to the subsystem. Hash-linking may be used where it adds verifiable integrity, but no document may promise “instant detection in the same microsecond” without measured proof.

## 5. Public-claim truth policy

The following phrases are **forbidden as factual public claims before evidence exists**:

- “impossible to hack”;
- “stronger than every AI”;
- “100% real impressions”;
- “zero fraud”;
- “always under 300ms”;
- “instant on every 2G network”;
- “microsecond escalation/delivery”;
- “zero downtime under every event.”

After exact-release evidence, public copy may state only the measured result, scope, date, environment, and test conditions.

Examples of acceptable evidence-backed statements:

- `5/5 authorized Red-Team campaigns PASS on release <SHA>, zero unresolved Critical/High findings.`
- `4,000,000 unique behavioral actors PASS under documented environment and cost ceiling.`
- `4,000,000 simultaneous active virtual users PASS under the certified Digital Twin environment.`
- `Shadow Ledger = 0 for the certified campaign.`
- `Android 20/20 and iOS 20/20 certification PASS.`
