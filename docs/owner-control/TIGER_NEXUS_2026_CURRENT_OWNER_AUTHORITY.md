# TIGER NEXUS™ 2026 — CURRENT OWNER AUTHORITY

**Status:** `CURRENT_ONLY / OWNER_BINDING / NO_FALLBACK / NO_IN_TREE_ARCHIVE`
**Effective decision:** 2026-08-29
**Domain:** primary product experience, sector-specialized social objects, contextual capabilities, and Pulse Vault interaction model.

## 1. Product identity

VVIP TIGER is a global sector-specialized social network. The primary surface is the social feed. Marketplace/sector discovery is a module inside that social product, not the default home and not a separate product identity.

The experience may use familiar social interaction patterns so users learn it immediately, but TIGER keeps its own branding, copy, visual identity, component geometry, information architecture, and product concepts.

## 2. Sector-only publishable purpose

TIGER does not provide a general-purpose public chatter feed. Every publishable Living Sector Object must bind to an activated TIGER sector and an approved sector intent.

Current canonical intent classes:

- `OFFER`;
- `NEED`;
- `SERVICE`;
- `OPPORTUNITY`.

Additional classes require a later explicit owner decision.

Ordinary eligible publication remains free. Pulse is optional paid visibility and never a publishing gate.

## 3. ONE FEED / ONE OBJECT / ONE PULSE

A Living Sector Object is created once and may participate in the feed, profile, search, sector discovery, saved items, messaging context, related discovery, and optional Pulse delivery.

The product must not require a second paid-post object or duplicate ad-creation workflow merely to promote an already eligible object.

## 4. Canonical creation entry

The primary composer copy is:

> **ماذا تعرض أو تحتاج؟**

Creation is progressive. The user starts with media, speech when available, or text; TIGER asks only for sector/policy-required missing information. The current product must not expose every possible field as an exam-like form before the user can begin.

## 5. TIGER Command

The persistent `☰` control is the universal **TIGER Command** entry.

Ordinary account actions and contextual privileged actions coexist in the same product. TIGER does not make a separate admin application the primary operating model.

Authority principle:

> **Same World — Different Authority.**

`صلاحياتي` is a first-class capability passport derived only from validated server-authoritative capability state. It exposes human-readable authority class, scope, status, and allowed actions. Null/unvalidated state exposes no privileged action.

## 6. Pulse Vault

Current Pulse reference purchase levels remain exactly:

- `PULSE_2` — 2 JOD;
- `PULSE_10` — 10 JOD;
- `PULSE_25` — 25 JOD;
- `PULSE_45` — 45 JOD.

Purchased visibility is a server-authoritative platform-service allocation and has **no product-time expiry**.

NEXUS exposes that allocation through a **Pulse Vault**. Visibility may remain unassigned until the authorized user chooses an eligible Living Sector Object and activates/allocates it under current server policy.

Pulse is not cash, a transferable wallet, buyer/seller settlement, escrow, or marketplace proceeds.

## 7. Delivery modes

Current NEXUS delivery modes are exactly:

- `NOW`;
- `SMART` — default;
- `PRECISE`.

They change delivery strategy only. They do not change purchased visibility quantity and cannot bypass sector, relevance, quality, trust, geography, inventory, legal, safety, or policy eligibility.

## 8. Verified delivery / ZERO-BURN

Consumption remains server-authoritative:

`RESERVE → SERVE → VERIFY → CONSUME`

A delivery unit consumes purchased visibility only when the current verified-delivery policy qualifies it. Fast scroll, hidden/background exposure, rejected automation/bots, invalid placements, suppressed duplicates/replays, failed reservations, and ineligible content consume zero purchased visibility.

Attention/engagement may improve future opportunity selection but does not silently multiply billing units.

## 9. Non-expiry and pause

No day/month/product timer expires Pulse because the user waited before using it or paused an allocation. Unconsumed visibility remains available subject only to current lawful refund/void/policy-ineligibility rules.

Technical TTLs for quotes, sessions, OTP, signed URLs, anti-replay, reservations, or caches are security/concurrency mechanisms, not product expiry.

Conflicting old material must be **deleted**, not hidden, renamed, moved to `legacy/`, `archive/`, `trash/`, retained behind a fallback, duplicated as a compatibility path, or preserved in current docs/tests/generated artifacts.

- Runtime;
