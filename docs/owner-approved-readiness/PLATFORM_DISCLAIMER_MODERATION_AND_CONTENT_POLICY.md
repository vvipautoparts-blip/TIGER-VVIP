# PLATFORM DISCLAIMER, MODERATION AND CONTENT POLICY

This policy draft supports product and operations alignment. It is documentation only and does not authorize Production or Backend changes.

## Platform Disclaimer

- The platform is for discovery and private communication.
- The platform is not a party to sale, payment, delivery, contract, or warranty.
- Users make private arrangements at their own responsibility.

## Prohibited Content

- Fraud and scam attempts.
- Identity impersonation.
- Abuse, harassment, or hate language.
- Fake products or deceptive pricing.
- Misleading categories or repeated spam.

## Reporting

- Users may report fraud, impersonation, abuse, fake products, misleading prices, wrong categories, and spam.
- Reports must capture a reference, reason, context, and timestamp.
- Evidence must be preserved before final action when possible.

## Moderation Actions

- Warning.
- Restriction.
- Suspension.
- Ban.

The action should match severity, recurrence, and evidence quality.

## Appeals

- Users may submit appeals with supporting evidence.
- Appeals should be reviewed by a different reviewer or higher authority when available.
- Final decisions must be logged with rationale.

## Privacy Boundary

- Do not expose passwords, OTPs, tokens, or management phone numbers.
- Do not expose private moderation notes publicly.
- Separate moderation records from Tiger Care support communication.

## Tiger Care Separation

- Tiger Care handles support, requests, and escalation intake.
- Moderation handles policy enforcement, evidence review, and account/content action.
- The two functions are related but not the same.

## User-Facing Reminder

- Respectful communication is required.
- The platform may act on unsafe or policy-prohibited content.
- This document is not a Production policy deployment.
