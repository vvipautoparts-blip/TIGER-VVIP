# COUNTRY EXPANSION DECISION FRAMEWORK

## Current Decision

- Jordan is the only approved launch country for now.
- No second country may be approved before the Jordan report and the first 60 days of data are reviewed.
- No expansion execution starts in this mission.

## Next-Country Scoring Inputs

The next country, when eventually evaluated, should be scored by:

- Real demand.
- Legal readiness.
- Local team readiness.
- Customer support readiness.
- Partner readiness.
- Operating cost.
- Language readiness.
- Payment readiness.

## Decision Rule

- Do not name a next country as approved in advance.
- Use the score only after the Jordan phase and owner review.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Jordan-only status is explicit.
- The next-country selection method is documented.
- No international rollout execution is implied.
