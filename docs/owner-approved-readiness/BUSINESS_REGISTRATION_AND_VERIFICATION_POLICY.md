# BUSINESS REGISTRATION AND VERIFICATION POLICY

This draft defines the owner-approved direction for business registration and staged verification. It is documentation only.

## Registration Requirement

Business registration or activity proof may be required for:

- Shops
- Service centers
- Dealers / Distributors
- Companies / Institutions

It is not a default requirement for:

- Buyer Viewer
- Buyer Standard
- Individual Seller
- Personal VIP

Service Provider cases are reviewed according to activity type, risk, and legal need.

## Verification Model

1. Phone verification.
2. Email verification.
3. Business document upload when needed.
4. Human review.
5. Administrative approve/reject with reason.
6. Audit trail.
7. Re-review when required.
8. No public exposure of sensitive documents.
9. No password, OTP, or token requests.

## Evidence And Privacy

- Capture only the documents and logs necessary for the review.
- Sensitive documents are not public content.
- Verification results should be visible as status, not as raw secret data.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Registration requirements are clear by account type.
- Verification is staged and reviewable.
- Sensitive data is protected.
- The policy does not claim full production enforcement.

## Boundary

This policy does not authorize Backend or Production implementation. It is a planning and alignment draft only.
