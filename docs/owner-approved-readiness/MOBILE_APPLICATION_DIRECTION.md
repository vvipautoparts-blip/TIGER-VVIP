# MOBILE APPLICATION DIRECTION

## Owner-Approved Direction

- One unified cross-platform application is approved for Android and iPhone.
- Web and PWA remain part of the product direction.
- Separate sector-specific apps are not approved.
- The three sectors remain filters inside the unified app.
- The exact framework is not approved yet.
- Flutter is one candidate, but the final framework decision waits for the technical spike and device testing.

## Product Meaning

- One app should serve buyers, sellers, business accounts, Tiger Care, moderation, and launch operations.
- The app should stay premium, simple, and mobile-first.
- This document does not claim production readiness.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Cross-platform direction is clear.
- Web/PWA continuity is preserved.
- Framework lock-in is deferred.
- No separate sector apps are introduced.
