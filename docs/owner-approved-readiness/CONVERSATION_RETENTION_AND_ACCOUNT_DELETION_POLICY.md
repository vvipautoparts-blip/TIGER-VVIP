# CONVERSATION RETENTION AND ACCOUNT DELETION POLICY

This document turns the owner-approved retention and deletion decisions into a planning draft only.

## Conversation Retention

- Conversations are retained for 90 days after the last activity.
- After 90 days, conversations are deleted or anonymized according to the active legal and technical policy.
- Evidence from reports, fraud cases, or security cases may be preserved separately when needed.
- Conversations are not reused for advertising or commercial targeting.

## Account Deletion

1. Disable the account immediately when a deletion request is confirmed.
2. Provide a 30-day recovery period.
3. After the recovery period, perform permanent deletion or anonymization.
4. Keep only the records required for security, reports, or legal obligations.
5. Do not expose deleted data to the public.
6. Clearly explain the difference between disablement and final deletion.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The 90-day message retention rule is documented.
- The 30-day deletion grace period is documented.
- Support text can explain the difference between disable and delete.
- No production implementation is claimed by this draft.

## Boundary

This policy does not authorize an active production data lifecycle change by itself.
