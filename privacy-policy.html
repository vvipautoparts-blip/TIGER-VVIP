Data protection

We use technical and organizational safeguards including access controls, bounded data paths, and security monitoring. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

User rights

  • Request access to eligible account data.
  • Request correction of eligible account data.
  • Request account/data deletion using the published Data Deletion Instructions.

Data retention

Data is retained only as needed for service operation, security, dispute handling, fraud prevention, and applicable legal obligations.
