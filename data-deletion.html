To request deletion of eligible VVIP TIGER NEXUS account data, use the verified support channel below. Final deletion is processed only after requester verification and the applicable security and legal-retention checks.

Submit a deletion request

  • Send the request to: vvipautoparts@gmail.com
  • Subject: Data Deletion Request

  • Requester identity is verified before eligible account data is deleted.
  • Deletion is handled according to applicable operational, security, fraud-prevention, dispute, and legal-retention requirements.
  • Data that must be retained for a lawful or security purpose is limited to the required scope and retention period.

Scope

Eligible account-associated profile, social, communication, and platform-service data is removed or de-identified according to the applicable deletion process. Some records may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, dispute handling, or legal obligations.
